Install · First setup · Using Meow · Updates & backups · Troubleshooting

Before you start

  • Supported device: this release is tested on the Portal identified by Android as omni, running Android 10 with ARM64. Portal TV, Portal Go, Mini and other Portal generations are not verified by this release; the installer stops on untested identities. It also ignores Android emulators.
  • Computer: Windows 10/11 on x64, or macOS 13+ on an Intel or Apple Silicon Mac. Windows on ARM needs separate validation; “any computer” is not a compatibility guarantee.
  • Cable: a USB cable that carries data, not only power. Keep the Portal connected to its normal power supply.
  • Space: have at least 1.5 GB free on the Portal; 2 GB or more is recommended for updates. Allow about 1 GB of free computer space for the installer and connection tools.
  • Internet: required the first time to download Google's connection tools, and later for your chosen online AI provider. Local USB installation itself does not need an AI account.

1. Open the installer

Windows: open Meow-Portal-Setup-Windows.exe. Mac: open Meow-Portal-Setup-Mac.dmg, drag Meow Portal Installer to Applications, then open it. The visual setup opens in your normal web browser. Its address starts with 127.0.0.1: this is the installer running on your computer, not a remote website.

The preview installers are not yet signed for public distribution. Windows or macOS may require an explicit approval to open them. Verify the download came from the owner's release and compare the supplied checksum. Do not disable your computer's security system. Public launch awaits code-signing and cross-platform acceptance testing.

If Android connection tools are already installed, select Prepare connection to reuse them. Otherwise read the Android SDK terms, tick the acceptance box, and select Prepare connection. The installer downloads the pinned Google tools archive directly from Google and verifies its SHA-256. No Android Studio installation is needed.

2. Enable the Portal connection

  1. On the Portal, open Settings → Debug → ADB Enabled. Enter its PIN if asked.
  2. Connect the USB data cable to the computer.
  3. Choose Check connection in the installer.
  4. On the Portal, tap Allow on “Allow USB debugging?”. Choose “Always allow” only on a computer you trust.
  5. If no device appears, toggle ADB Enabled off and on, reconnect, and check again.
The Portal must approve debugging. An installer cannot turn it on before authorization. This tool does not flash firmware, unlock a bootloader, disable verification or stop system updates. If your firmware has no Debug option, consult Meta's Portal setup instructions; don't install a random firmware image.

3. Install Meow

Select your Portal and click Install Meow, or Update / repair Meow if it's already installed. The installer checks compatibility, available storage, and the app's integrity. Keep the cable connected until it reports completion. Meow should open on the Portal.

If multiple devices are connected, select the intended Portal. The installer never selects an unrelated phone or changes your other apps. The package is separate from the phone/Play Store edition.

First setup on your Portal

  1. Choose your companion. Pick a look and character, then use “Fit to my screen.”
  2. Set the pace. Fresh Portal setups default to a 30 FPS avatar target. Heat and idle policies may lower the actual rate. Settings → Avatar lets you choose another target. Existing settings are kept when updating.
  3. Choose a background or Desk. The illustrated Desk gives you a room with a cat, notes, computer and accessible bottom navigation.
  4. Connect an AI provider. Follow Meow's AI setup and connection test. Enter keys only in Meow on the Portal. Provider subscriptions, API access, credits and free-model limits are separate from this installer. The installer never needs your keys.
  5. Choose permissions. Microphone, camera, notifications and other access are optional and explained on the device. A permission is not granted by this computer installer.
  6. Optional home screen. If you want Meow to open as your home app, select it in Android's chooser. Otherwise keep your existing launcher. You can change this later in Android's default-app settings.

Updates, backups and recovery

Get the newest installer from the owner's releases, open it, reconnect the Portal and choose Update / repair Meow. The included APK is verified before installation and again on the device. Android checks signing compatibility. Your existing package is updated in place; app data is not deliberately cleared.

Before a major update: use Meow's backup/export controls to create an encrypted backup and save it somewhere outside the Portal. Keep its password separately. This computer installer cannot read Meow's private encrypted keys and does not claim to make a full private-data backup for you.

If Android reports a different signing key, stop and back up first. The installer will not uninstall Meow as a workaround. Downgrades are blocked. To remove Meow later, use Android's app settings; uninstalling removes its local data, so export a backup first.

Privacy and what changes

The installer communicates with the selected Portal over USB. It reads device identity, Android version, available storage and Meow's installed version; installs the verified APK; and opens Meow. Its web interface is restricted to your computer and protected by a temporary session token. There is no installer telemetry, account sign-in, or API-key collection. Google receives the tools-download request. In-app providers have their own data practices.

Debugging grants a trusted computer powerful access. Disconnect when finished; disable ADB Enabled on the Portal if you don't need it. The installer does not silently make Meow your launcher, enable wireless debugging, disable updates, bypass the firmware verifier, or grant privacy permissions.
